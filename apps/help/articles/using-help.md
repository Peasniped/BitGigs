Help comes in two shapes: a **popup** for the page you're on, and this **full
manual** for reading through.

## The popup

Press **F1** anywhere, or click the floating **?** button in the corner. It
opens beside the page — including on top of an open dialog — and shows the
articles for *that* page first, so you rarely have to search at all.

Links inside an article keep you in the popup rather than navigating away, and
**Full manual** at the bottom opens whatever you're reading as a full page.

## Search

The search box at the top of the popup matches as you type, across article
**titles**, **keywords**, **summaries** and the **article text** itself. Titles
rank highest; a match found in the body shows the sentence it was found in, so
you can see why a result is there.

- Type more than one word to narrow it — every word has to appear somewhere.
- **↑/↓** move through the results, **Enter** opens the highlighted one.

## The manual

The tree on the left is the table of contents; **‹ Previous** and **Next ›** at
the bottom of an article walk it in reading order. New here? Start with
[Getting started](/help/getting-started/).

## Turning it off

The floating **?** button can be hidden under
**More › Settings › [Display](/settings/?tab=display)**. F1 and **More › Help**
keep working either way.
