A **workplace** is one job you work. Everything else — shifts, pay terms,
payslips — hangs off a workplace.

## What a workplace holds

| Piece | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Icon & name** | How the workplace shows up across calendars and charts. |
| **Contract** | A named container for your employment (it has **no dates** of its own). |
| **Term sets** | The dated pay terms inside a contract — salary or hourly rate, hours, etc. |

## Customising the look

Open a workplace and use **Customize appearance** to set three things:

- **Icon** — either a built-in symbol or your own uploaded logo. They are
  alternatives, not a pair: picking a symbol replaces an uploaded logo, and
  uploading a logo clears the symbol. PNGs are cropped to a square; SVGs can be
  recoloured first.
- **Background colour** — the avatar circle behind that icon.
- **Accent colour** — the workplace's own colour. It tints a built-in symbol and
  **themes the workplace's own pages**: on that workplace's dashboard, its edit
  and pay-terms forms and its approve-shifts page, the buttons, links, switches
  and highlights all use this colour instead of the app accent. Leave it unset
  and those pages keep the app accent.

The icon and accent are what you'll see on the planning calendar, on shift chips
and in analytics.

> A workplace's active span is **derived** from its term sets — see
> *Contracts & pay terms* for how that works.
