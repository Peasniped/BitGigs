A **workplace** is one job you work. Everything else — shifts, pay terms,
payslips — hangs off a workplace.

| Piece | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Icon & name** | How the workplace shows up across calendars and charts. |
| **Contract** | A named container for your employment. It has **no dates** of its own. |
| **Term sets** | The dated pay terms inside a contract — salary or hourly rate, hours, etc. |

A workplace's active span is **derived** from its term sets, not stored — see
[Contracts & pay terms](/help/contracts-and-terms/).

## The workplaces page

Each card shows the job's hours and earnings for the month; click through for
that workplace's own dashboard, calendar and payslips. A workplace with an accent
colour of its own **themes its own pages** — buttons, links and highlights follow
that colour instead of the app accent, so it's always obvious whose job you're
looking at.
