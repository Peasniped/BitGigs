A **workplace** is one job you work. Everything else — shifts, pay terms,
payslips — hangs off a workplace.

## What a workplace holds

| Piece | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Icon & name** | How the workplace shows up across calendars and charts. |
| **Contract** | A named container for your employment (it has **no dates** of its own). |
| **Term sets** | The dated pay terms inside a contract — salary or hourly rate, hours, etc. |

## Customising the look

Open a workplace and use **Customize appearance** to upload an icon. PNGs are
cropped to a square; SVGs can be recoloured first. The icon is what you'll see
on the planning calendar and analytics.

> A workplace's active span is **derived** from its term sets — see
> *Contracts & pay terms* for how that works.
