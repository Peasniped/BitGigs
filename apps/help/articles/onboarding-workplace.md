Your first job: a **name** is all that's required. Once you've typed one an
optional **contract label** appears — useful if you'll want to tell contracts
apart later (each stint at an employer gets its own), but fine to leave empty.

You're also asked whether to send this job's shifts out as
[calendar invites](/help/calendar-invites/). Answer **No** if you're not sure; it
can be turned on later from the contract page. Saying **Yes** without a mail
server configured adds a short, skippable email-setup step after this one.

Everything else — icon, colours, more workplaces — is added later from the
**Workplaces** page; see
[Creating & editing workplaces](/help/workplace-editing/). The pay side of this
job comes on the next step.

> Nothing here is written to the database until **Finish**. See
> [First-time setup](/help/first-time-setup/).
