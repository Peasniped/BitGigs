Your first job: a **name** is all that's required. Once you've typed one, an
optional **contract label** appears — useful if you'll want to tell contracts
apart later (each stint at an employer gets its own contract), but fine to
leave empty.

Everything else — icon, colours, more workplaces — is added later from the
**Workplaces** page; see
[Creating & editing workplaces](/help/workplace-editing/). The pay side of
this job comes on the next step.

> Nothing is written to the database until **Finish** on the last step, and
> going back keeps what you've typed.
