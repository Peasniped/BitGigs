A fresh BitGigs install walks you through four steps before the app opens up.
The step circles at the top show where you are — a **yellow** circle is a step
you've already filled but stepped back from; click any filled circle to jump
around.

## 1 — Account

Whoever reaches a fresh install first would own it, so this step first asks
for the **setup key** — a one-time code printed in the server's log (and
written to `instance/setup_key.txt`). Only the person who installed BitGigs
can read it, which is what proves you're the owner.

Then you create the single owner account: your **email** (which is also your
username), a **display name** used for greetings, and a password — or, if the
server has single sign-on configured, you can create the account through your
identity provider instead (you'll see the identity on a confirm page before
anything is created).

## 2 — Tax profile

The personal numbers from your **forskudsopgørelse** at skat.dk —
trækprocent, personfradrag and so on. See
[Tax profiles](/help/a/tax-profiles/) for what each field means.

## 3 — Workplace

Your first job: a name is enough, and you can give its contract an optional
label. Everything about a workplace can be changed or added later.

## 4 — Pay terms

How that job pays — hourly or salaried, rate, hours, vacation type. See
[Contracts & pay terms](/help/a/contracts-and-terms/).

## Nothing is saved until Finish

Steps 2–4 are kept as a draft: you can go back, log out, even close the
browser, and your entries are still there when you return. Only pressing
**Finish** on the last step writes everything — so a half-done setup never
leaves half-done data behind.
