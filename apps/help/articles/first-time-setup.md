A fresh BitGigs install walks you through four steps before the app opens up.
Each has its own article:

1. **Account** — [claim the instance](/help/onboarding-claim/) with the setup
   key, then [create the owner account](/help/onboarding-account/) (email +
   password, or through your identity provider).
2. **[Tax profile](/help/onboarding-tax/)** — the numbers from your
   forskudsopgørelse.
3. **[Workplace](/help/onboarding-workplace/)** — name your first job.
4. **[Pay terms](/help/onboarding-terms/)** — how that job pays, and
   **Finish**.

## Moving around

The step circles at the top show where you are. A **yellow** circle is a step
you've already filled but stepped back from — click any filled circle to jump
between steps.

## Nothing is saved until Finish

Steps 2–4 are held as a draft: go back, log out, even close the browser, and
your entries are still there when you return. Only **Finish** on the last step
writes everything to the database, together — a half-done setup never leaves
half-done data behind.
