A fresh BitGigs install walks you through six steps before the app opens up.
Each has its own article:

1. **Account** — [claim the instance](/help/onboarding-claim/) with the setup
   key, then [create the owner account](/help/onboarding-account/) (email +
   password, or through your identity provider).
2. **[Start](/help/onboarding-start/)** — import an existing BitGigs export, or
   set everything up from scratch.
3. **[Tax profile](/help/onboarding-tax/)** — the numbers from your
   forskudsopgørelse.
4. **[Workplace](/help/onboarding-workplace/)** — name your first job.
5. **[Pay terms](/help/onboarding-terms/)** — how that job pays.
6. **[Review](/help/onboarding-review/)** — what setup has, what it still
   needs, and **Finish**.

## Two ways through

If you pick **import** on step 2, the file usually supplies steps 3–5 for you
and you go straight to Review. If it only covers part of what's needed, Review
says so and you can either import another file or fill the rest in by hand —
the two can be mixed freely.

Choosing from scratch simply walks steps 3–5 in order.

## Moving around

The step circles at the top show where you are. A **green** circle is done —
including steps an import already satisfied. A **yellow** circle is a step
you've filled but stepped back from. Click any filled circle to jump between
steps.

## Nothing is saved until Finish

Steps 3–5 are held as a draft: go back, log out, even close the browser, and
your entries are still there when you return. Only **Finish** on Review writes
them to the database, together — a half-done setup never leaves half-done data
behind.

Importing is the one exception: a file is written as soon as you confirm it, so
Review shows you what actually landed rather than a preview.
