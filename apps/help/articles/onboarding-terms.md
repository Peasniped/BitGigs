This step describes how the job from the previous step pays: hourly or salaried,
the rate or monthly salary, weekly hours, payroll period, tax card, vacation type
and any extras.

- Field-by-field guide: [Filling in pay terms](/help/pay-terms-editing/).
- The Danish concepts: [Danish pay concepts](/help/danish-pay-terms/).

Unsure about a value? Enter your best guess — terms are
[date-versioned](/help/contracts-and-terms/), so they can be corrected or
extended later from the workplace's page without losing history.

**Continue** takes you to [Review](/help/onboarding-review/), which lists what
setup has and what it still needs.

> Nothing here is written to the database until **Finish**. See
> [First-time setup](/help/first-time-setup/).
