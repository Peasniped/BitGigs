The planning calendar is a cross-workplace month grid. Use it to sketch shifts
ahead of time and to confirm the ones you've actually worked.

## Planned vs. approved

- **Planned** shifts are your intentions. Analytics uses them to project income
  for the current and future months.
- **Approved** shifts are what really happened. Approving a planned shift turns
  it into a real record that counts toward payroll.

## Handy things

- Click a day to add a shift; click an existing chip to edit it.
- **Drag a workplace card** from the strip at the top onto a day to plan a
  shift there.
- Times are always entered in **24-hour** format.
- Overlapping shifts are highlighted in amber and flagged before you save.

## Seeing your other commitments

If you've connected a personal calendar (Google, Fastmail, iCloud, …), a **Show
my calendar** button appears at the top. Turn it on to overlay that calendar's
busy times as muted read-only chips, so you don't plan a shift on top
of something you already have on — a clash is flagged in amber just like two
overlapping shifts. Set it up under
[Settings → Calendar](/settings/?tab=calendar); the full walk-through is in
[Calendar integration](/help/calendar-integration/).

## Going faster

For bulk work — stamping a default shift across a month, copying a shift to
several days, clearing days — open [Power Edit](/help/power-edit/). What the
chip colours and symbols mean is covered in
[Shift types](/help/shift-types/).
