The planning calendar is a cross-workplace month grid. Use it to sketch shifts
ahead of time and confirm the ones you've actually worked.

## Planned vs. approved

- **Planned** shifts are your intentions. Analytics uses them to project income
  for the current and future months.
- **Approved** shifts are what really happened, and count toward payroll.

## Handy things

- Click a day to add a shift; click a chip to edit it.
- **Drag a workplace card** from the strip at the top onto a day to plan a shift
  there.
- Times are always entered in [24-hour format](/help/numbers-and-times/).
- Overlapping shifts are highlighted in amber and flagged before you save.
- **Drag a chip to another day** to move it. If it lands on top of something
  else, you're told straight away and offered **Cancel move** or **Edit shift**.

## Calendars

Connect a personal calendar and a **Show my calendar** button appears, overlaying
your busy times so you don't plan a shift on top of something you already have on
— see [Reading a calendar into planning](/help/calendar-overlay/). Shifts can also
be emailed out as [calendar invites](/help/calendar-invites/).
