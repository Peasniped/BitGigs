A shift's **type** says what kind of paid time it is (see
[Shift types](/help/shift-types/)); its **state** is how it looks on the
calendar right now. These are the markers below the type colours in the legend
in this help panel.

| On the chip | State | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Dashed outline | **Planned** | Drafted but not yet approved — still editable on the planning grid. |
| Solid fill | **Approved** | A confirmed shift that counts in your pay and totals. |
| ☕ cup icon | **Has a break** | An unpaid break is subtracted from the paid hours. |
| ✉ envelope | **Calendar invite sent** | An invite has been emailed for this shift. |
| Greyed-out | **Different payroll period** | Belongs to a neighbouring payslip — read-only here. |

## Greyed-out shifts

The planning calendar pads each month so you can see every workplace's whole
**payroll period**, so a few days at the top and bottom belong to the
**neighbouring period**. Shifts on those days show up **greyed-out** — they
belong to a different payslip, so here they're read-only: clicking does nothing,
and dragging one just reminds you it's a different period. When you use **Send
invites** for a month, greyed-out shifts are **skipped** — you'll send them when
you open the month they actually belong to.

> Sending invites is covered in
> [Calendar invites](/help/calendar-integration/); approving planned shifts in
> [Approving shifts](/help/approving-shifts/).
