A shift's **type** says what kind of paid time it is (see
[Shift types](/help/shift-types/)); its **state** is how it looks on the calendar
right now. These are the markers below the type colours in the legend in this
help panel.

| On the chip | State | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Dashed outline | **Planned** | Drafted but not yet approved — still editable on the grid. |
| Solid fill | **Approved** | A confirmed shift that counts in your pay and totals. |
| ☕ cup icon | **Has a break** | An unpaid break is subtracted from the paid hours. |
| Blue ring + ✉ | **Invite sent** | A [calendar invite](/help/calendar-invites/) has gone out for this shift. |
| Amber ring + ✉ | **Invite out of date** | The shift changed after its invite was sent. |
| Red ring + ✉ | **Invite failed** | The mail server refused it — see [When an invite doesn't send](/help/invite-problems/). |
| Greyed-out | **Different payroll period** | Belongs to a neighbouring payslip — read-only here. |

A chip's **colour** is its workplace's accent, so what the ring and outline tell
you never competes with whose job it is.

## Greyed-out shifts

The grid pads each month so you can see every workplace's whole
[payroll period](/help/payroll/), so a few days at the top and bottom belong to
the **neighbouring** one. Shifts there are read-only: clicking does nothing, and
dragging one just reminds you it's a different period.

**Send invites** skips them too — you'll send them when you open the month they
actually belong to.
