A one-month snapshot across all your workplaces. Use the arrows around the month
title to move; **Today** jumps back to the current month.

## The stat cards

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Planned** | Estimated gross/net for shifts still *planned* this month. |
| **Earned** | Gross/net for shifts already **approved**. |
| **Total** | Planned + earned combined. |
| **Hour Goal** | Approved + planned hours against your goal, when a workplace has one. Hover the bar's segments for exact numbers. |

## Today's shift banner

If you have a shift today, a banner shows where and when. While it's still
unapproved it asks **"Did you arrive early?"** with the planned start filled in —
correct it and press the check to save. With several shifts today it asks about
each in turn.

If the time you enter would land at or after the shift's end, the banner flags it
and offers **Adjust shift**, opening the full editor with your arrival time
carried over so you can move the end time too.

## Review & Approve

Once planned shifts reach today's date they wait for confirmation. A banner counts
them; **Review & Approve** opens a modal where you can adjust times and type
inline, tick the shifts that really happened, and **Approve Selected**.

Nothing is saved until you approve — and the modal warns before letting unsaved
edits be thrown away. Full detail: [Approving shifts](/help/approving-shifts/).

## Workplace cards & calendar

Each card shows that job's hours, average hours/week and gross earned for the
month; click through to its own page. Below, the calendar shows the month at a
glance, with a note flagging shifts that fall in a neighbouring calendar month but
belong to **this month's [payroll period](/help/payroll/)**.
