The dashboard is a one-month snapshot across all your workplaces. Use the
arrows around the month title to move; the **Today** button jumps back to the
current month.

## The stat cards

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Planned** | Estimated gross/net for shifts still *planned* this month. |
| **Earned** | Gross/net for shifts already **approved**. |
| **Total** | Planned + earned combined. |
| **Hour Goal** | Approved + planned hours against your goal (only shown when a workplace has an hour goal). Hover the bar's segments for the exact numbers. |

## Today's shift banner

If you have a shift today, a banner shows where and when. While the shift is
still unapproved the banner asks **"Did you arrive early?"** with the planned
start time filled in — correct it if you clocked in earlier or later, and
press the check to save. With several shifts today it asks about each in turn.
If the time you enter would land at or after the shift's end, the banner flags
it and offers **Adjust shift**, opening the full editor with your arrival time
carried over so you can move the end time too.

## Review & Approve

Once planned shifts reach today's date they wait for your confirmation. A
banner counts them; **Review & Approve** opens a modal where you can

- adjust start/end times and the shift type inline,
- tick the shifts that really happened and **Approve Selected**,
- or open the full editor with the pencil (your unsaved inline edits carry over).

Nothing is saved until you approve — and the modal warns you before it lets
unsaved edits be thrown away.

## Workplace cards & calendar

Each workplace card shows its hours, average hours/week and gross earned for
the month — click through to that workplace's own page. Below, the calendar
shows the month at a glance; a note above it flags shifts that fall in a
neighbouring calendar month but **belong to this month's payroll** (payroll
periods don't always match calendar months).
