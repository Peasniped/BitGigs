Rate history charts how your pay rate has moved over time, per workplace.

- Each change in a term set shows as a **step** on the line.
- Compare workplaces side by side using the same filter bar as the projection.

It's a quick way to see the effect of a raise, or to confirm a new term set
took effect on the date you expected.
