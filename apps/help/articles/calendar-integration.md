BitGigs talks to calendars in two **independent** directions. Both are optional
and off until you set them up under
**[Settings → Calendar](/settings/?tab=calendar)**.

| Direction | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Reading in](/help/calendar-overlay/)** | Overlays a personal calendar's busy times on the planning grid, so you never plan a shift on top of something you already have on. |
| **[Sending out](/help/calendar-invites/)** | Emails a calendar invite for each shift, so colleagues — and your own calendar — stay in sync. |

Neither sends your BitGigs data anywhere except where you explicitly point it:
reading in only *fetches* a URL you paste, and sending out rides the mail server
you configured on the [Email tab](/help/email-and-password-reset/).
