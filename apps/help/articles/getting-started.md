Welcome to **BitGigs** — it tracks your shifts across workplaces and estimates
your Danish net pay.

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **[Workplaces](/help/workplaces/)** | Each job you work, with its pay terms. |
| **[Planning](/help/planning-calendar/)** | A month calendar for planning shifts and approving worked ones. |
| **[Payroll](/help/payroll/)** | Estimated payslips per period — tax, holiday pay, pension, the lot. |
| **[Analytics](/help/analytics-projection/)** | Income projection and rate history across all your jobs. |

The usual rhythm: plan shifts ahead on the calendar, approve them once you've
worked them, and let payroll and analytics do the arithmetic.

## Worth reading early
