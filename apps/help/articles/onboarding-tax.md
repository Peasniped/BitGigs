This step asks for the personal numbers BitGigs uses to estimate what's withheld
from your pay: **trækprocent**, monthly **personfradrag**, **AM-bidrag** and (if
you're a church member) **church tax**.

Copy them straight from your **forskudsopgørelse** — log in at
[skat.dk](https://skat.dk) and open your preliminary assessment.

- What each field means: [Tax profiles](/help/tax-profiles/).
- New to the Danish terms? [Danish pay concepts](/help/danish-pay-terms/).

Don't worry about getting it perfect. Tax profiles are **dated**, so when your
numbers change you add a new one under **More › Tax Profiles** — and you can
correct this one there too.

> Nothing here is written to the database until **Finish**; going back or logging
> out keeps what you've typed. See
> [First-time setup](/help/first-time-setup/).
