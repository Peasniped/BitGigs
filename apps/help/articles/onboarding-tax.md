This step asks for the personal numbers BitGigs uses to estimate what's
withheld from your pay: **trækprocent**, monthly **personfradrag**,
**AM-bidrag** and (if you're a member of the church) **church tax**.

Copy them straight from your **forskudsopgørelse** — log in at
[skat.dk](https://skat.dk) and open your preliminary assessment.

- What each field means: [Tax profiles](/help/tax-profiles/).
- New to the Danish terms? [Danish pay concepts](/help/danish-pay-terms/).

Don't worry about getting it perfect: tax profiles are **dated**, so when your
numbers change (typically each new year) you add a new profile under
**More › Tax Profiles** — and you can correct this one there too.

> Your input here isn't saved to the database yet — everything is written
> together when you press **Finish** on the last step. Going back (or even
> logging out) keeps what you've typed.
