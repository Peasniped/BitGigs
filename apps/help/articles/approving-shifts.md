Approving confirms the shifts you actually worked so they count toward payroll.

## The flow

1. Review the listed shifts. Fix any start/end/type inline if reality differed
   from the plan.
2. **Select** the ones to approve.
3. **Approve selected** writes them as real records.

## Good to know

- Inline edits and your row selection only reach the server when you press
  **Approve selected** — so BitGigs warns you if you try to leave with unsaved
  changes or selections.
- The **pencil** opens the full shift editor with your unsaved inline values
  carried over, so nothing is lost.
- A row with impossible times (end before start) can't be approved until fixed.
