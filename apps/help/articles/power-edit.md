**Power Edit** (the ⚡ button top-right of the planning calendar) opens a
toolbar of bulk tools, so filling or clearing a month takes seconds instead of
one dialog per shift. One tool is active at a time; press **Esc**, click
outside the calendar, or use the toolbar's **×** to step back out.

## The tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fast Create** | Click (or drag) a workplace card, then click days to *stamp* that workplace's **default shift** onto them — no dialog. |
| **Copy** | Click an existing shift to copy it, then click days to paste it there. |
| **Delete** | Click shifts to delete them instantly. |
| **Delete All** | Appears while Delete is active. Click a workplace card to delete **all** its shifts this month, or click a day to clear that whole day. |

Stamping respects the rules the normal dialog enforces: days outside the
payroll period or outside the contract's active span are refused with a
warning instead of silently creating a misplaced shift.

## Default shifts

Fast Create stamps each workplace's **default shift** — set it via the small
**gear** on the workplace card at the top of the page: start and end time,
break minutes, and shift type. If a workplace has no default configured yet,
you'll be prompted when you try to stamp with it.

## The workplace cards

The card strip at the top isn't just for Power Edit:

- Drag a card onto a day to plan a shift there any time.
- Each card shows the workplace's **planned + approved hours** for the month,
  and a progress bar toward its hour goal when one is set.
