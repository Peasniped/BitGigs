The vacation overview aggregates the holiday pay you accrue across your
workplaces, so you can see it in one place — a sanity check on what should land
in FerieKonto, and a basis for planning paid time off.

Which model a job uses is set per term set (**vacation type** in
[Filling in pay terms](/help/pay-terms-editing/)):

- **Feriepenge via FerieKonto** — typical for hourly work: a share of your
  earnings is paid in, and you claim it when you take vacation.
- **Accrued paid vacation** — typical for salaried work: you earn vacation days
  and keep your salary while on holiday, plus **ferietillæg**.

Booking a **vacation** [shift type](/help/shift-types/) draws down the accrued
balance on contracts that work that way.

As with the rest of payroll, these figures are estimates. The percentages and
rules behind them are explained in
[Danish pay concepts](/help/danish-pay-terms/).
