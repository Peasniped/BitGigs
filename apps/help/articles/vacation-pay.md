The vacation overview tracks the holiday pay you accrue across your workplaces.

- Danish holiday pay (**feriepenge**) accrues as a share of your earnings.
- The overview aggregates what you've built up so you can see it in one place.

Use it to sanity-check what should land in **feriekonto**, and to plan paid
time off. As with the rest of payroll, figures here are estimates.
