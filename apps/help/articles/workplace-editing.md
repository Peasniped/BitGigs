## Adding a workplace

**Workplaces › Add** asks for very little: a **name** (the short URL slug is
generated for you). That's deliberate — a workplace is just the container;
what it pays lives in its [contract's term sets](/help/a/contracts-and-terms/),
which you add next.

## The contract

Each workplace gets a **contract**: a named folder for its pay terms. Its only
editable field is an optional **label** (useful later if you re-join the same
employer on new terms — each stint gets its own contract). A contract has
**no dates of its own**: when it starts and ends follows entirely from the
dates on its term sets, and two contracts at one workplace can't overlap in
time.

## Customising the look

On the workplace's page, **Customize appearance** controls how the job shows
up everywhere — calendars, cards, charts:

- **Colour** — pick from swatches, a colour wheel, or a hex value.
- **Icon** — upload an image. PNGs and photos go through a square cropper;
  **SVGs** first open a recolour step, where you can remap the graphic's
  colours (per colour, or one overall tint) with a live preview before
  cropping.

## Deactivating vs. ending

Don't delete a workplace you've stopped working at — its history feeds
payroll and analytics. Instead **end the contract** by putting an
`effective until` date on its last term set; the workplace then counts as
inactive from that date, and your past months stay intact.
