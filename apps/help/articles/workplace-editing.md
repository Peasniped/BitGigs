## Adding a workplace

**Workplaces › Add** asks for very little: a **name** (the short URL slug is
generated for you). That's deliberate — a workplace is just the container; what
it pays lives in its [term sets](/help/contracts-and-terms/), which you add next.

Each workplace gets a **contract**, a named folder for those terms. Its only
editable field is an optional **label**, useful if you re-join the same employer
on new terms — each stint gets its own contract.

## Customising the look

On the workplace's page, **Customize appearance** controls three things:

- **Icon** — either a built-in symbol or your own uploaded logo. They're
  alternatives, not a pair: picking a symbol replaces an uploaded logo, and
  uploading a logo clears the symbol. PNGs and photos go through a square
  cropper; **SVGs** first open a recolour step where you can remap the graphic's
  colours (per colour, or one overall tint) with a live preview.
- **Background colour** — the avatar circle behind that icon.
- **Accent colour** — the workplace's own colour. It tints a built-in symbol and
  **themes the workplace's own pages**: its dashboard, its edit and pay-terms
  forms, its approve-shifts page and its payslip editor use this colour for
  buttons, links, switches and highlights. Leave it unset and those pages keep
  the app accent.

Pick colours from swatches, a colour wheel, or a hex value. The icon and accent
are what you'll see on the planning calendar, on shift chips and in analytics.

## Deactivating vs. deleting

Don't delete a workplace you've stopped working at — its history feeds payroll
and analytics. Instead **end the contract** by putting an `effective until` date
on its last term set. The workplace counts as inactive from that date, and your
past months stay intact.
