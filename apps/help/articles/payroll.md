Payroll turns your approved shifts into an **estimated payslip** for each pay
period — gross pay, tax, holiday pay, pension and the net you take home.

## How it fits together

- A **period** covers one month at a workplace (bounded by the active term set,
  falling back to the full month).
- Generating a period builds **payslip lines** from your hours and pay terms.
- BitGigs applies your **tax profile** to estimate withholding.

These are *estimates* to help you plan — always check against your real payslip.

See the child articles for **payslip details**, **vacation pay** and
**commuting** deductions.
