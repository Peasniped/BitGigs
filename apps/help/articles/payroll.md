Payroll turns your approved shifts into an **estimated payslip** for each pay
period — gross pay, tax, holiday pay, pension and the net you take home.

- Generating a period builds **payslip lines** from your hours and pay terms.
- BitGigs applies your [tax profile](/help/tax-profiles/) to estimate
  withholding.

These are *estimates* to help you plan — always check against your real payslip.

## A payroll period is not a calendar month

Each period covers **one payday at one workplace**, bounded by the term set
active at the time. Many hourly jobs run e.g. the **20th → 19th**, so the shifts
you worked 20–31 July are paid in **August** and belong to the August period.

This one rule shows up all over the app:

- The dashboard flags shifts sitting in a neighbouring calendar month that
  belong to *this* month's payroll.
- The planning calendar greys out shifts from a neighbouring period, and **Send
  invites** skips them — see [Shift states](/help/shift-states/).
- [Analytics](/help/analytics-projection/) buckets income by period, which is why
  its year total is *income paid in that year* — the basis Danish tax uses.

Workplaces paid on the 1st are unaffected: their period **is** the calendar
month. You set the start day per term set — see
[Filling in pay terms](/help/pay-terms-editing/).
