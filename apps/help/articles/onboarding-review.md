The last step, and the only one that finishes setup — whether you imported a file
or typed everything in.

## What BitGigs needs

Two things, both shown with a tick or a cross:

- **Tax details** — one tax profile.
- **A workplace and its pay terms** — a job, and how it pays.

That's the whole requirement. Anything missing gets a **Fill this in** button
that jumps to the right step, and **Finish setup** stays disabled until both are
ticked.

Each line also says where it came from: *imported from your file*, or *will be
created when you finish*.

## Workplaces that still need pay terms

If your import file logged shifts against a workplace it never actually
described, we create that workplace so the shifts aren't lost — but it has no
pay terms yet, so those shifts can't be priced.

Review lists your workplaces with a tick or a warning each, names any that still
need terms, and **Finish stays disabled** until they're filled in. The button
takes you to the pay terms step with a blank form for that workplace; if several
are waiting, you'll be sent back for the next one.

## Your account

Review also shows the account you created in step 1 — display name, email, and
your password as dots. **Edit** and **Change** open the same dialogs the settings
page uses, so you can correct a typo in your email or change your password
without leaving setup.

## Starting over

**Start over** clears everything setup has gathered — imported workplaces, pay
terms and shifts, your tax details, and anything typed into the steps — and
returns you to [Start](/help/onboarding-start/). It asks for confirmation first,
and it leaves your account alone: you stay signed in and won't need the setup key
again. Handy if you imported the wrong file.

## Shifts don't block anything

If your file brought shifts, they're listed too — but only so you can see they
arrived. Setup finishes perfectly well with zero shifts; you'll add them as you
work. Nothing in this section can stop you.

## Filling a gap

Two ways, and you can mix them:

- **Import another file** — useful when your data is split across exports, or
  when the first file was made without tax details. Later imports see what the
  earlier ones created, so you can map a renamed workplace onto an existing one.
- **Fill this in** — go to the step and type it.

## Finish

Pressing **Finish setup** writes anything still held as a draft — all at once, so
a failure can't leave half a setup behind — and opens your dashboard.

Imported data is already saved by this point (that happens when you confirm the
import), which is why Review shows real counts rather than a preview.
