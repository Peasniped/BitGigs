A payslip breaks a period into **lines** — earnings, deductions and
contributions.

## What you can do

- **Add / edit / delete lines** for anything not derived automatically.
- **Reorder** lines to match how your employer lays them out.
- **Recalculate** to rebuild the derived figures after you change shifts or
  pay terms.

## Automatic pieces

Holiday pay (feriepenge / feriekonto), **fritvalg**, pension and ATP are
estimated from your hours and terms. Tax withholding comes from the **tax
profile** effective for the period.
