Task queue

One-off work handed to the scheduler by the app — sending a calendar invite, for instance. Nothing retries by itself (a re-sent invite would land twice), so a failed row waits here until you press Retry or clear it. Rows disappear on their own once the queue is long enough; clear them here to tidy up sooner.
